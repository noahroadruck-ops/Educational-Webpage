SpaceX Future Plans

SpaceX's future plans and goals.

Reusable rockets

One of SpaceX's most important acheivement has been reusable rockets. In 2015, they succesfully landed a falcon 9 first stage booster. This helped show that orbital rockets could be recovered after a launch rather than being destoryed.

SpaceX continued improving their landing technology and rockets. Falcon 9 rocket boosters eventually reached the goal of landing on land and on ships. This helps their goal of making space travel cheaper.

Dragon Spacecraft and Human flight

In May 2020, SpaceX successfully carried crewed missions using their Dragon spacecraft. They carried several NASA astronauts to the International Space Station.

This led NASA to certify SpaceX and the Dragon spacecraft for crew transportation. This helped further push the idea that SpaceX as a private company was critical for space exploration.

Milestones

  1. 2015- Falcon 9 first stage booster lands
  2. 2017- Falcon 9 booster lands on a droneship
  3. 2020- Crew Dragon carries NASA astronauts to ISS

Starship

Starship is SpaceX's largest rocket and is being actively developed. Its' goal is to be fully reusable and their solution to space travel beyond Earths orbit.

Starship also goes beyond just being a reusable ropcket, as SpaceX has succeessfuly developed a landing tower that catches starship and its' 33 rocket engine booster. They aim to make this reusable, but also launched in mass repeadetly, and SpaceX has acquired new land to build dozens of towers.
